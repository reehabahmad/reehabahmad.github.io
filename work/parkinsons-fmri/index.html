Lead research project · Riphah International University · May 2024

Medication-related connectivity in Parkinson's disease

Resting-state fMRI analysis of how medication changes functional connectivity in Parkinson's disease.

Role
Lead
Signals
Resting-state fMRI
Methods
ROI-to-ROI · seed-to-voxel
Tools
SPM · CONN · MATLAB

Research question

How does dopaminergic medication reshape the brain's functional networks at rest?

Pipeline

  1. rs-fMRI
  2. SPM preprocessing
  3. CONN
  4. ROI-to-ROI · seed-to-voxel

I used two complementary views: ROI-to-ROI, which compares coupling between predefined regions, and seed-to-voxel, which maps everywhere in the brain a chosen region couples with.

What I learned

This was my first deep dive into neuroimaging, and it set habits I now use with EEG: fix your preprocessing choices early, look at the data at every stage, and treat each connectivity map as a hypothesis, not a picture.
